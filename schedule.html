📌 Attendance Requirements

Minimum 70% Attendance Required to pass the course

This isn't just a rule—we want to see you and code together! Your presence matters for group learning, collaboration, and making the most of your vocational education.

  • Regular attendance builds professional habits
  • You'll miss key concepts and peer collaboration if absent
  • Team dynamics and group projects depend on your participation

⏱️ Lateness & Early Departures

Lateness is okay if there's a valid reason

We understand life happens. Just keep us in the loop:

  • Text Tim to let him know if you'll be late
  • Provide a reason (traffic, personal issue, etc.)
  • Aim to catch up on missed content

Early Departures

If you need to leave early, get approval from the trainer first. Only trainers can dismiss class early.
