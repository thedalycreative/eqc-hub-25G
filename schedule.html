💡 Pro Tips for Success

  • Arrive on time: Missing the first 10 minutes means missing typing test practice and attendance
  • Stay engaged: Typing test is not just a warm-up; it builds professional speed for real-world work
  • Use breaks strategically: Don't skip breaks or work through them; your brain needs rest to absorb information
  • Lunch is sacred: Step away from code during lunch. You'll come back refreshed and creative
  • Ask questions: If you don't understand something, ask immediately. Don't let confusion build
  • Collaborate: Pair programming and peer learning is encouraged. You learn better together
  • Document your code: Comments help you remember your logic and help others understand your work
  • Keep your workspace clean: Leave the classroom as you'd like to find it for the next class
  • If you'll be late or absent: Text Tim or your trainer for the day at 0414 265 050 before class starts
  • Communication: No posting - questions go to group chat (be constructive)

🆘 If Issues Arise During Class

💻 Technical Problem

If your code won't run or you're stuck on a bug, raise your hand immediately. Tim or your trainer for the day or a classmate can help you debug.

😟 Personal Issue

If you're feeling unwell or need to step out, let Tim or your trainer for the day know. We can work out accommodations.

🚨 Emergency

If there's a medical or safety emergency, call 000 immediately, then notify Tim or your trainer for the day.
